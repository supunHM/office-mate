HR KNOWLEDGE BASE

Employee Leave Policy

All employees are entitled to 15 days of annual leave per year.

Sick Leave:
- Up to 10 days per year
- Medical certificate required for absences over 2 days

Vacation Request Process:
1. Submit leave request form
2. Manager approval required
3. Minimum 2 weeks notice for vacation

Payroll Information:
- Salary paid on the last working day of each month
- Pay slips available through employee portal

Contact HR department for any questions regarding leave or payroll.
